# No AI Collisions Pro Edition - Patch Notes

## 1.5.0.0
- AutoDrive helpers that touch another vehicle (from any side, moving or stopped) now pass through it until they are 2 m apart, instead of getting stuck. Queues of helpers waiting at the same AutoDrive point still wait in line as normal.
- Any helper (AutoDrive, Courseplay or the base game helper) that touches a tree now drives straight through it instead of stopping first.
